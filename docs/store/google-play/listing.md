## Store listing

**App name** (30): Vicu

**Short description** (80):

A focused task manager for your Vikunja server. Offline, widgets, reminders.

**Full description** (4000):

Vicu is a clean, focused task manager for Vikunja, the open-source to-do server you host yourself or rent from Vikunja Cloud. Your tasks stay on your server; Vicu makes them fast and pleasant to work with on your phone.

ORGANISED AROUND WHEN THINGS HAPPEN
• Inbox catches everything new.
• Today shows what is due today, with overdue tasks on top.
• Upcoming lists the days ahead.
• Anytime is your open backlog, grouped by project.
• Logbook keeps what you finished.
• Review reminds you to look at each project now and then.

QUICK ADD THAT UNDERSTANDS YOU
Type "Call Ana friday 3pm #Personal @call p2" and Vicu sets the date, time, project, label and priority as you type. Repeats like "every 2 weeks" work too.

MADE FOR THE PHONE
• Swipe right to complete, swipe left to schedule.
• Drag to reorder, or sort a project by due date, priority or title.
• Home screen widgets for Today, Inbox, Upcoming, any project or a custom list; tap to complete.
• Reminders per task, a morning and afternoon summary, and routines with their own reminders.
• Share anything to Vicu to turn it into a task, with files attached.
• Works offline: changes wait on the phone and sync when you are back online.

CUSTOM LISTS AND ROUTINES
Build your own lists from projects, labels and date windows. Track daily or weekly routines. Both sync with your other devices.

SHARE WITH YOUR HOUSEHOLD OR TEAM
Assign tasks, comment with mentions and see who a shared project is shared with.

ONE APP EVERYWHERE
Vicu also runs on iPhone, iPad and the desktop, and every version reads your data the same way.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account with us, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Vicu only talks to the Vikunja server you choose. Open source: github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile

You need a Vikunja server (version 2.4 or newer). Sign in with your password, single sign-on (OIDC) or an API token.

Vicu is an independent app and is not affiliated with the Vikunja project.

## App content

- **Privacy policy**: the URL above.
- **App access**: All or some functionality is restricted. Instructions for the reviewers:
  - Name: Vikunja demo server
  - Username: `demo`, password: `demo` (the public demo account of try.vikunja.io, published in the server's own welcome message)
  - Instructions: "On the first screen enter the server address https://try.vikunja.io and tap Continue. Choose "Sign in with password", enter username demo and password demo, and sign in. Pick any project as the Inbox and tap Complete Setup. try.vikunja.io is the public demo server of the Vikunja project; it is reset at irregular intervals, so its tasks may change between visits."
- **Ads**: No, the app contains no ads.
- **Content rating**: Category "All other app types". Answer No to violence, sexual content, language, controlled substances, gambling. Users can interact (comments in shared projects), but only with people on the same self-hosted server whom they share projects with; no public communication, no location sharing, no purchases.
- **Target audience**: 18 and over (not designed for children). Not appealing to children.
- **News app**: No.
- **Data safety**:
  - Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.** The app sends data only to the server the user chooses, run by the user or a host the user picked, not to the developer; the developer receives no data.
  - (If Play insists on declaring transmission off the device: Personal info > Name, Email address and App activity > Other user-generated content, collected not shared, processed for App functionality, required, encrypted in transit when the server uses https.)
  - Data deletion: users delete data on their own Vikunja server; signing out removes it from the device.
- **Government app**: No. **Financial features**: None. **Health**: None.
- **Exact alarm permission** (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`): the user grants it; used for task reminders at the time the user set.
