# Vicu 2.1.0 for Android and iOS

Vicu 2.1.0 brings Vicu to iPhone and iPad. It is the same app as on Android, from the same code: the same lists, editor, quick add, routines, custom lists, review and sharing, reading your Vikunja data by the same rules as Android and the desktop app. Both apps also get optional task counts in the drawer and a few fixes.

On iPhone and iPad, Vicu comes through TestFlight first, before the App Store. If you find a rough edge, please [open an issue](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Android/issues).

### Vicu on iPhone and iPad

- Every screen of the Android app, with iOS gestures and system settings: swipe back from the edge, Dynamic Type, light and dark appearance.
- Sign in with your password (with two-factor), an API token or single sign-on. Some providers, Google among them, do not allow sign-in inside an app; use your password or an API token there.
- **Reminders and daily summaries**, with Mark Complete and Snooze on the notification, and Complete and Skip for routines. iOS keeps at most 64 notifications waiting, so Vicu plans the soonest ones ahead and tops them up whenever something changes.
- **Widgets**: a Tasks widget for Today, Inbox, Upcoming, Anytime, any project or custom list (choose it in Edit Widget), and a Routines widget, both with tap-to-complete. A completion from a widget is sent the next time Vicu runs.
- **Quick add from anywhere**: an Add task control for Control Center, the Lock Screen and the Action Button (iOS 18), "Add a task in Vicu" with Siri, and Add task when you hold the app icon.
- **Share to Vicu** from any app: a small sheet with the title, notes and attachments; the task lands in your Inbox.
- Attachments from Photos and Files, opened with Quick Look and shared with the share sheet.
- Offline changes are kept and sent when the connection is back, also from the background when iOS allows it.
- Vicu collects no data. Your sign-in is kept in the Keychain on this device only.

What works differently on iOS is described in [docs/ios.md](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Android/blob/main/docs/ios.md#differences-from-android).

### New on Android and iOS

- **Task counts in the drawer**: turn on Show task count (Settings, Projects) to see how many open tasks each project has. It is independent of Show project progress, so the drawer can show the rings, the counts, both or neither.

### Fixes

- When you scroll back to the top of a list, pulling down now always opens the large title first; only a further pull refreshes. Before, a slow pull often refreshed and left the title small.
- The summary times in Settings follow your phone's 12 or 24 hour clock.

- Today no longer opens scrolled past your routines when they load after your tasks.
- Setup keeps a comfortable width on tablets instead of stretching across the screen.
- The calendar in the When sheet and in the reminder picker starts the week on Monday, as the rest of Vicu and the desktop app do, whatever your phone's region. Swipe it or use the arrows to change the month; days of the weeks around the month can be picked too.

### Known issues

- Undo of a repeating task that was completed while offline does not put its dates back.
