# Vicu 2.4.0 for Android and iOS

Vicu 2.4.0 lets you share a routine with someone you already share a project with, gives each routine a colour of its own, and keeps syncing on a server whose search is broken. Update the desktop app to 2.4.0 at the same time: it shares routines too.

### Share a routine

- **Share with**, in the routine editor, lists the people of the projects you already share. Pick one and the routine moves into that project; Vicu never creates or shares a project for you. People who can only view a project are listed as "Can view only". When the person is in more than one of your projects, choose which one keeps the routine.
- A tick or skip by either of you shows for both, and a finished time says who did it ("Done by Maya"). Two people ticking at the same moment both count.
- Members who can edit the project tick, skip and edit the routine; view-only members only look. Only you, the owner, can stop sharing, archive or delete it.
- **Your own choices**: as a member, turn the routine's reminders off for you, or hide it. Hidden routines are listed in Settings, where you can show them again. Both follow you to your other devices.
- Deleting or archiving a project that holds a shared routine says so first, even with "Confirm before deleting" off.
- Android: a shared routine's reminder looks at the routine again before it rings, so a time someone else already ticked stays quiet. iPhone and iPad plan their reminders after each refresh.

### Routine colours

- Give a routine a colour in the editor (Color, at the end): none, or one of the project colours. Its ring and icon on the Routines screen and in Today take the colour, and so does its ring in the Routines widget on Android and iOS.
- Light colours such as yellow darken just enough to read on a light background. A colour picked in the desktop app shows as a swatch of its own.

### Fixes

- **Syncing with a server whose search is broken.** A Vikunja server on ParadeDB whose `pg_search` extension is older than its library (a newer image pulled without running `ALTER EXTENSION pg_search UPDATE`) answers every search with "unexpected error occurred". The first sync after signing in failed with that error and loaded no tasks. Vicu now syncs your tasks, custom lists, settings and routines on such a server; searching in Vicu works again once the server is fixed. ([#51](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/issues/51))
