# Vicu 2.3.1 for Android and iOS

Vicu 2.3.1 rebuilds quick add as one sheet that opens with the keyboard, keeps list titles compact next to the menu, and makes Vicu available on Android tablets again. Nothing synced changed: the desktop app 2.3.0 works with it as it is.

### Quick add

- **Opens with the keyboard.** The sheet comes up together with the keyboard, with the title ready to type, and stays where it is while you work in it. Before, it climbed after the keyboard, and on Android the keyboard could stutter on the way up.
- **One line of chips**: When, Remind and Priority, plus tags, a project and a repeat once they are set. A chip opens its choices where the keyboard was: When with times and a calendar, reminders and places, priority, tags (with New tag), projects and repeat. Tap the title to get the keyboard back without the sheet moving.
- **Expand** grows the same sheet to full height, with a row for each property, notes and a checklist. Checklist items become subtasks of the new task.
- Nothing is saved until you tap Add.

### Lists

- The title of a list sits next to the menu button and stays put; the bar changes colour as the list scrolls under it. Today shows its date as a small line under the title.

### Fixes

- A long press on a row you can drag (Inbox and project tasks, projects, lists and labels in the drawer, Settings > Projects) picks it up without also opening it.
- Android: Google Play offers Vicu again on devices without GPS or a microphone, mostly tablets, which 2.3.0 left out. On those devices location reminders and voice input stay off.
